Who We Are

Today’s Tasks is a lightweight to-do web app focused on privacy and clarity. We do not require accounts and we keep your personal tasks on your device by default.

Related: About · Why digital to-do lists? · How to use the app

Information We Collect

  • Site analytics: We do not embed an analytics service or collect page-view analytics ourselves. Hosting and advertising providers may process technical request data under their own privacy policies.
  • Ad data: Third-party ad partners may process limited data to deliver and measure ads. See Third-Party Services.

Local-Only Tasks

Your to-do items are stored in your own browser’s LocalStorage. By default, we do not transmit task content to our servers.

To remove local data anytime, you can clear site storage for todays-tasks.com in your browser settings or use the app’s reset options (see How to Use).

Third-Party Services (Advertising)

Google AdSense may display ads on selected informational pages. We do not load the AdSense ad script on the main to-do app, interactive tools, document-processing screens, app directories, or mobile-app download pages, so ads are kept away from those controls and store buttons.

When an ad request is made, Google may process technical and advertising data such as your IP address, browser and device details, page address, cookies or advertising identifiers, and ad interactions to serve, limit, and measure ads. Google's handling of that data is governed by its own policies:

  • Google Ads & Technologies
  • Google Cookies
  • AdSense Privacy & Messaging
  • Have I Been Pwned privacy policy

For the Data Leak Checker, password mode sends only the first five characters of the password’s SHA-1 hash to Have I Been Pwned; the full password stays in your browser. Email mode opens the HIBP site with the address you entered, so that address is sent directly to HIBP only if you choose to continue.

Cookies & Controls

Google and other third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and measurement. You can restrict cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect other website features.

  • Google Ad Settings lets you manage Google ad personalisation; it does not replace a consent choice or withdraw consent collected through a consent message.
  • If Google presents a consent message on this site, use its controls to accept, reject, or revisit your choices.
  • You may also use private browsing or your browser's tracking-protection controls.

Data Security

We apply industry-standard safeguards to protect site traffic and minimize data collection. Because task content is stored locally, you control it on your device.

Your Rights & Choices

  • Access & Deletion: Clear site data in your browser to remove tasks and local settings.
  • Opt-out: Manage ad personalization via Google Ad Settings.
  • Contact: Have a privacy question? Contact us.

Children

Today’s Tasks is intended for general audiences and not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

International Users & Ad Consent

Google may process ad-request data in regions where it operates. For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Google requires a Google-certified consent management platform (CMP) to collect consent before personalised ads are served.

The consent message is controlled through the publisher's AdSense Privacy & Messaging settings, not by a separate banner in this site's static code. Whether that message is published and correctly targeted must be verified in the AdSense account. If a Google consent message appears, use its controls to make or revisit your choices; Google Ad Settings alone is not a substitute for withdrawing consent through the CMP.

For questions or a privacy request, use our Contact page. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal.

What We Keep, and For How Long

Because Today's Tasks does not require an account, there is no server-side profile to retain. Concretely:

  • Your tasks: kept in your browser's LocalStorage until you clear site data, switch browser, or use the Reset action in the app. We never have a copy.
  • Ad-serving data: retained by Google AdSense in line with their own policies. We never receive your individual click or impression history.
  • Email you send us: kept in our inbox so we can follow up on your question. If you want it deleted, ask and we will delete it.

What We Deliberately Do Not Do

It's often clearer to list what a site doesn't do than what it does. Today's Tasks does not:

  • Require, create, or store user accounts.
  • Send your task content, task counts, or task names off your device.
  • Sell, rent, or trade any personal information.
  • Use fingerprinting techniques to identify visitors across sites.
  • Run cross-site tracking pixels of our own (third-party ad partners may, subject to their own policies — see above).
  • Send push notifications, marketing email, or product re-engagement messages from a server.

If any of this changes — for example, if a future feature needs server-side sync — this policy will be updated before the feature ships, and the relevant section will say so explicitly.

Policy Updates

When this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. Material changes will be summarized here.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us via the Contact page.
