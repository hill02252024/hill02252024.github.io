Bead Shop: 拼豆工坊 — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Summary

Bead Shop has no account system and no product analytics backend. Game progress, shop settings, achievements, and custom bead patterns are stored locally on your device. We do not upload them to our server. Optional advertising is provided by Google AdMob, which may process device and ad-related information as described below.

Information stored on your device

  • Shop name, game progress, coins, achievements, patterns, and settings.
  • Notification preferences and whether tutorials have been seen.
  • Local diagnostic messages used to recover from an app error.

This information stays in app storage unless you remove it yourself. Cloud save is not currently available. Uninstalling the app or erasing its data may permanently delete your progress.

On iOS, App Tracking Transparency is requested before personalized advertising where required. You can decline and still use the game. Consent choices and regional requirements may limit the data available to the SDK. See Google's advertising policies.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your card number or store account password. The app receives product availability and entitlement status so it can enable a purchase or restore an eligible non-consumable item.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Notification titles, times, and preferences are not uploaded to us. You can change or disable each category in Settings or through your operating system.

Children

Bead Shop is a general-audience game and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Your choices and deletion

  • Delete local game data by removing the app or using your device's app-data controls.
  • Change ad personalization and tracking permissions in your device settings.
  • Reset or delete your advertising identifier through the controls provided by iOS or Android.
  • Disable notifications, sounds, and haptics in the game or system settings.

We do not maintain an account database that you need to delete. Data handled by Google is governed by Google's own controls and policies.
