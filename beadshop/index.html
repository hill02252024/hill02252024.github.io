Frequently asked questions

How do I start playing?

The first launch opens a small bead level. Pour beads from a jar, tap the matching cells on the board, and finish the picture. Your first finished piece opens the shop-management mode. You can switch between bead levels and your shop at any time.

Does the whole game need an internet connection?

No. The levels, shop simulation, custom patterns, and saved progress work offline. Internet is only needed for optional advertisements, store purchases, and restoring purchases. Those features fail gracefully and do not block normal play.

Does the shop mode use energy?

No. Bead levels use energy, but the shop-management mode never spends it. You can always return to your shop even when level energy reaches zero.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the level map or Settings and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID or Google Play account that made the purchase. Non-consumables such as ad removal and eligible passes can be restored. Coins, energy, and other consumables are not restored.

Why are some store items unavailable?

Products must be active in App Store Connect or Play Console. Check your connection, confirm that you are signed in to the correct store account, then reopen the shop and retry. You can keep playing and using in-game coins even when the store is unavailable.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Cloud save is not currently available. Progress and custom patterns are stored locally on this device. Reinstalling or erasing app data may permanently remove them. Non-consumable purchases are separate and can be restored from the store. Do not erase your app data if your progress matters to you.

Can I turn off sounds, vibration, or notifications?

Yes. Open Settings to change sound, haptics, language, and each notification category. Local reminders can be disabled individually or all at once.

Where is the privacy policy?

Read it at todays-tasks.com/beadshop/privacy/. It explains which data stays on your device and which data the optional advertising SDK may process.
