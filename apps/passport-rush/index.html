Passport Rush: a travel-themed arcade runner

Race through 30 destinations, dodge airport hazards and learn a different rule in each country. It is an arcade game for travel fans—not a real-world trip planner.

▶ View on Google Play

The live listing is for Android. Google Play currently labels the app as containing ads and in-app purchases; check the listing for its latest details before installing.

How a run works

Passport Rush is built around short arcade runs. Tap to move between lanes, avoid airport hazards and collect boarding stamps before the gate closes. The destination changes the challenge, so a run in one country does not play exactly like the next.

  • 30 destinations, 30 modifiers. The Play listing describes country-specific changes such as speed boosts, fog, magnets and faster combo scoring.
  • Daily missions. A country target gives each day a new objective and a postcard to add to the collection.
  • Power-ups and characters. Pick up items during a run and try travellers with different passive abilities.
  • Shareable scorecards. The game can generate a boarding-pass-style card with the run's score and destination to share with friends.

A travel theme, not a travel utility

The country names, city scenes and passport stamps give the game its travel personality. They are part of the arcade setting; the app does not provide maps, flight details, visa information or live travel recommendations.
