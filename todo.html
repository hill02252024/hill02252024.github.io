Today's Tasks – One List for the Work You Actually Do

Every work day has the same three jobs: a meeting, time out of the office, and IT support. This page keeps one list per date for those three, in your browser. No account. Yesterday stays where you left it.

Write today ↓

Built for a day that always has the same three parts

A general to-do list makes you invent categories every morning. This one does not. Meetings, going out, and IT support are already the lanes. You only write the line: who, where, or what you fixed.

開會 Meetings

One line: time, who, what was decided. Tick it when you leave the room.

外出 Out

Where you went and what you did there. The day still makes sense when you are back at the desk.

IT support

User, problem, fix. Unfinished jobs can be copied onto the next morning instead of being retyped.

One day, kept

Each date is its own page of notes. Midnight opens a blank today. It does not delete yesterday.

Copy the day

One button copies that date as plain text, so you can paste it into a message or an email.

On this device only

Nothing in the list is uploaded. See the privacy policy.

How a morning goes

  1. Open today. If an IT job was still open yesterday, press「由上一日帶未完成」. Do not retype it.
  2. Write one line in the right lane. 開會 for the 10:00 client call. 外出 for the site visit. IT support for the printer reset.
  3. Tick it when it is done. It stays on that date, crossed out, so the day still shows what happened.

        Who it's for

        A day that always contains a meeting, a trip out, and IT support. Write the fact, not a project name. "10:00 with accounts, agreed the printer stays" is a line. "Follow up" is not.

        IT jobs that run overnight stay unfinished. Next morning, bring them forward instead of starting from a blank memory. Meetings and site visits usually finish the same day, so they stay on that date as the record.

        Frequently asked questions

        Do I need an account or to install anything?

        No. Open the page and write today's work. No sign-up, no install, no email.

        Where is my data stored?

        In your browser's localStorage, on your device only. The list is not uploaded. If you clear browser storage or switch browsers, the days go with it.

        Does it work offline?

        Yes, after the page has loaded once. Add and tick items without a network. Each day stays on this device.

        How should I use the three lanes?

        開會 for time, people, and the decision. 外出 for where you went and what you did. IT support for the user, the problem, and the fix. One line each. Tick it when it is done. It stays on that date.

        What happens at midnight?

        The page opens on the new date with an empty list. Yesterday is still there — use the date to read it. Unfinished lines can be copied onto today with「由上一日帶未完成」.

        Can I fix a line I ticked by mistake?

        Yes. Press the arrow on that line to mark it open again, or the cross to delete it.

        Is it really free?

        Yes. No in-app purchases, no premium tier. The site shows ads to cover hosting; see the privacy policy.
