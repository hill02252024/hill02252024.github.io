Built for one job: get today done

Most to-do apps ask you to set up workspaces, tags, and projects before you can write one line. This page does not. One input. Three lanes. A Completed section for the day you are looking at. There is nothing to configure.

Meetings, time out of the office, and support work all fit in those three lanes. Write the fact — who, where, or what you finished. The lanes stay the same every day, so you are not renaming the page to match the job.

No account needed

Open the page and start. The list stays in this browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

Three lanes

High Priority for what has to happen. Due Today for what is actually due. General for the rest.

At least a month

Each date keeps its own list. The calendar opens any day in the month, and earlier months stay too.

Works offline

After the first load, add and tick items with no network. The days stay on this device.

Same on a phone

One input, three lanes, a month grid. The same flow on a phone or a desk.

Privacy by default

Task text is not uploaded. See the privacy policy.

How it works in three steps

  1. Type a task. One line, one outcome. “Draft the Section 2 outline” moves. “Work on the report” sits there.
  2. Pick a lane. High Priority for the short must-do list. Due Today for time-bound work. General for everything else. General is the default.
  3. Press Add. Tick it when it is done. It stays on that date, under Completed, so the day is still a record tomorrow.

          Who it's for

          The same three lanes, whatever the day contains. A meeting is a line in Due Today. A site visit is a line in High or Due Today. A support job is a line in whichever lane matches how urgent it is. You do not need a lane named after the job.

          Work days. Three High Priority items by the time you start. Everything else waits in General until it earns a spot. If yesterday still has open lines, bring them forward instead of retyping.

          A month you can still read. Open last Tuesday from the calendar when someone asks what you did. Completed stays on that date. Unfinished stays open until you tick it or bring it to another day.

          Frequently asked questions

          Do I need an account or to install anything?

          No. Open the page and write. No sign-up, no install, no email.

          Where is my data stored?

          In this browser only. The list is not uploaded. Clearing site data, or switching browsers, removes the days.

          Does it work offline?

          Yes, after the page has loaded once.

          How should I use the lanes?

          High Priority for the short list that has to happen. Due Today for what is actually due. General for the rest. Write meetings, trips, and support as ordinary lines.

          How far back can I read?

          Every day you write stays in this browser. Use the calendar for this month, and the month arrows for earlier months. Opening the page tomorrow shows tomorrow; it does not delete today.

          Can I undo a tick?

          Yes. In Completed, press the arrow to put the line back in its lane, or the cross to delete it.

          Is it really free?

          Yes. No account and no paid tier. The site shows ads to cover hosting; see the privacy policy.

          邊個用

          欄名唔跟工作種類改。會議寫一行落今日要做。出街寫一行落重要或者今日要做。支援寫一行，緊急先放重要。唔使另開一欄叫支援。

          返工。開工之前，重要最多三行。其餘先放一般。上一日仲有未完成，用「由上一日帶未完成」，唔使重打。

          一個月後都翻到。有人問你上星期二做過咩，喺日曆撳嗰一日。已完成留喺當日。未完成會一直開住，直到你打勾，或者帶去另一日。

          常見問題

          使唔使登入或者安裝？

          唔使。開頁就寫。冇註冊、冇安裝、唔使電郵。

          資料放喺邊？

          只喺呢部瀏覽器。清單唔會上傳。清咗網站資料，或者換瀏覽器，日子會一齊冇。

          離線用唔用得？

          第一次載入之後用得。

          三欄點用？

          重要放一定要做嘅短名單。今日要做放到期嘅。其餘放一般。開會、出街、支援都係普通一行。

          可以翻返幾遠？

          寫過嘅每一日都留喺呢部瀏覽器。呢個月用日曆，再早用月份箭咀。聽日開頁見到聽日，唔會刪走今日。

          打錯勾點算？

          喺已完成按箭咀，行會返去原來嗰欄。交叉就係刪除。

          係咪免費？

          係。冇帳號、冇付費版。網站有廣告支付託管，詳見私隱政策。
