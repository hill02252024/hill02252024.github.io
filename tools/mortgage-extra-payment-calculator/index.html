How the comparison works

The standard payment is calculated from principal, fixed annual rate and term. The accelerated schedule uses that same payment, adds your monthly extra, applies the annual extra every 12th month and applies the lump sum in your selected month. Extra money goes to principal after the month's interest.

Interest saved is total interest with no extras minus total interest with your plan. Time saved is the difference in monthly payments. A final extra payment is capped so it cannot exceed the remaining balance.

What this page deliberately leaves out

This is principal-and-interest math, not a complete housing budget. It excludes property tax, insurance, HOA dues, PMI/MIP, lender fees, prepayment penalties, recasting and adjustable rates. Check whether your servicer requires special instructions to apply extra money to principal.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include tax, insurance or PMI?

No. This page compares principal and interest only. The Android app provides the fuller PITI and mortgage-insurance estimate.

When is the annual extra applied?

Every 12th payment month. The one-time lump sum uses the month you select.

Is this a loan offer?

No. It is an educational estimate, not financial advice or a loan offer. Your lender's actual schedule and documents control.
